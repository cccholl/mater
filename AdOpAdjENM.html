Module 5: Common Error Clinic (B1+/B2+)

Frequent Error (B1+/B2+) Correct Form Pedagogical Rationale
The presentation was very fascinating. The presentation was absolutely fascinating. Extreme adjectives cannot be preceded by gradable adverbs like 'very'; they require absolute intensifiers like 'absolutely'.
We stayed in a five-stars hotel. We stayed in a five-star hotel. Compound modifiers preceding nouns maintain a singular numerical form.
